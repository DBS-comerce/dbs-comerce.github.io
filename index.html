Cove

Hold a seashell to your ear. From anywhere.

Cove is an ambient-noise app built around a simple idea: every seashell hums a slightly different sea. Pick a shell, hit play, and Cove synthesises the matching color of noise — brown, pink, gray, white, or blue — for as long as you want it. No streams, no buffering, no internet required after first launch.

The five shells

Each shell is paired with the noise color whose spectrum best matches its acoustic personality. Audio is synthesised on device the first time you use a shell, then cached for offline play.

Conch

Classic shell-to-ear ocean roar, warm and deep.

Scallop

What's in it

  • Plays in the background. Lock the screen, switch apps, the sea keeps going.
  • Works offline. Audio is synthesised once, then cached locally.
  • No account. Open the app and listen — nothing to sign up for.
  • No ads, no trackers. Cove doesn't sell or share anything about you.
  • Bluetooth, AirPlay & CarPlay friendly. Routes correctly to whatever output you're using.
  • Free. No subscription, no in-app purchases.
